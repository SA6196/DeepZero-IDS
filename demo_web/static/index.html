💡 How to read this dashboard (Plain English)

1. Reconstruction Error as Anomaly Score: The Autoencoder was trained strictly on verified benign normal traffic. When normal flows pass through, the model easily compresses and reconstructs them (yielding low scores). When unexpected or malicious zero-day traffic passes through, the model struggles to reconstruct the unfamiliar patterns, resulting in a significantly higher reconstruction error.

2. False-Alarm Budget (q): The alarm threshold τ is calibrated against held-out normal traffic so that only a controlled budget q (e.g. 1% or 5%) of benign flows produce false alarms.

3. Sensitivity Trade-Off: Increasing q (e.g., from 0.01 to 0.05 or 0.10) lowers the threshold line, catching more stealthy or low-volume attacks at the cost of a slightly higher benign false alarm rate.
